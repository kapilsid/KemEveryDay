Kemistri Everyday — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Kemistri Everyday is an educational chemistry game published by EdBetter. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

Kemistri Everyday does not ask you to create an account or provide an email address. To provide Daily Champions, Firebase Authentication creates a random anonymous player identifier. The app also stores the public nickname you choose and your completed daily scores in Google Firebase. Please do not use your real name as your nickname.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob between Daily boards, never during play. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, and crash and diagnostic information. In the UK, EEA and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Data stored on your device

To work offline, the app keeps your current games, Daily Board scores and streak history, chemical combinations waiting for review, Element a Day guesses, and your app settings on your device. Unrecognised chemical combinations waiting for review are not uploaded.

When Firebase is available, the app sends your anonymous player identifier, public nickname, completed Molecule Board and Science Uncovered scores, discovery counts, content version and submission time to Firebase for the Daily Champions daily and weekly leaderboards. Other signed-in anonymous players can see the nickname and leaderboard result, but not your private player profile. Historical daily and weekly results may be retained to operate and protect the leaderboards.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account settings, and EdBetter cannot access it.

Deleting your data

You can remove individual review items in the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes local data but does not automatically delete an already submitted Firebase leaderboard entry. To request deletion of Firebase leaderboard data, contact EdBetter at the address below and include the nickname and date concerned. Because the account is anonymous, EdBetter may need additional information to identify the correct entry.

Children

The app is designed to avoid collecting a child's name or contact details. Players should use a made-up nickname rather than a real name. Daily Champions stores the limited technical and score data described above.

Educational disclaimer

Kemistri Everyday describes known substances and everyday chemistry for learning. Do not attempt experiments with hazardous substances; follow the safety notes shown in the app and seek adult or expert supervision.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides anonymous authentication, database storage, abuse protection and leaderboard delivery. Google AdMob provides advertising and consent controls. These providers may process technical data under their own privacy terms.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
