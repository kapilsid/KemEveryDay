Build real molecules, meet a new element every day and discover the chemistry hiding in ordinary life.

DAILY MOLECULE BOARD
Everyone gets the same 5×5 board of atoms each day. Link neighbouring atoms to make known substances such as water, table salt and carbon dioxide. Find 10 different substances in 20 moves to complete the mission, then keep going for a higher score.

DAILY STREAK
Play every day to build your streak. A calendar shows each day you played, which days you completed the mission, and your best scores.

ELEMENT A DAY
Meet one element from the periodic table each day. See its atomic number, period, group and family, learn something useful about it, and explore where it sits in the table.

EVERYDAY CHEMISTRY
Why does cutting an onion make you cry? Why does a cake rise? Take a guess, then reveal the chemistry underneath, with a simple equation, something to try and a safety note.

MADE FOR LEARNING
• Over 120 known substances with short, accurate notes
• Honest chemistry: a formula tells you which atoms are present, not always a single structure, and the app says so
• Unrecognised combinations are saved for review rather than called impossible
• Hints and shuffles when you are stuck
• Reduced-motion setting and screen-reader descriptions

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no adverts, no analytics and no internet connection needed. Your progress stays on your device.
